Biography

I am a Ph.D. student at the University of Science and Technology of China, affiliated with the Shanghai Innovation Institute. I received my B.Eng. in Automation from Hunan University in 2022.

My research explores how AI systems reason with visual information, check claims against evidence, and use external tools to solve unfamiliar problems. Recent projects span scientific reasoning agents, multimodal fact-checking, image forensics, and reinforcement learning for open-ended tasks.
