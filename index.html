Hi, I’m Fanrui. I am a fifth-year Ph.D. student at the University of Science and Technology of China (USTC), jointly trained at the Shanghai Innovation Institute (SII). I am advised by Zheng-Jun Zha, Kaipeng Zhang, and Junjun He.

My research focuses on agentic post-training and coding agents. I am interested in how agents learn from interaction and feedback to solve complex, open-ended tasks. My recent work explores reinforcement learning, training data synthesis, and tool use, with the goal of building agents that can reason, search, and write code more reliably.
